## The style, in words

- Sunlit tropical Caribbean in warm afternoon light: clear skies, strong golden sun, soft shadows.
- Saturated colour. Deep sapphire open sea turning turquoise and aqua over shallows, crisp white
  foam along coasts.
- Lush deep greens, palms and dense jungle canopy, golden cream sand, warm grey-brown rock.
- Spanish colonial towns: whitewashed and pale ochre plaster, terracotta tile roofs, warm beige
  stone walls and forts, weathered wooden docks.
- Ships: dark warm brown hulls, cream canvas sails, bright flags.
- Stylised semi-realism like a modern city-builder (*Anno 1800*, *Port Royale 4*): handcrafted,
  clean readable shapes, moderate detail. Never photoreal, never gritty.

Model sheets: plain light grey background, a three-quarter hero view, then orthographic top,
front and side at one scale, a human silhouette for scale, and six colour swatches.
